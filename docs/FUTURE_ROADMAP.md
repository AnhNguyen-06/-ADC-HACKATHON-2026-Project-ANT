# Future Roadmap & Post-MVP Extensions — Project ANT

The following items are explicitly **excluded from the hackathon MVP** to preserve demo stability, explainability, and adherence to Rule 1 (MVP First) and Part 33.

---

## Post-MVP Extensions (Tier 1: Next Milestone)
1. **Multi-Floor Routing & Elevator Integration**:
   - Graph edges with vertical floor transitions.
   - Integration with smart elevator APIs or floor level visual beacon tags.
2. **Semantic Landmark Audio Expansion**:
   - Dynamic route cues anchored to static landmarks: "Turn right just after the water dispenser."
3. **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) / UWB Beacon Fusion**:
   - Secondary radio-frequency coarse positioning to seed initial room-level localization before camera tags are sighted.

---

## Long-Term Research & Speculative Innovations (Tier 2)
1. **Wearable Smart Glasses Integration**:
   - Hands-free chest-mounted or glass-mounted camera with bone-conduction audio transducers.
2. **Cognitive Load Adaptive Audio**:
   - Drawing inspiration from the team's multimodal dyslexia and eye-tracking research: adjusting speech cadence, verbosity, and repetition frequency based on environmental noise or user hesitation.
3. **Edge SLAM & LiDAR Fusion**:
   - Real-time 3D spatial meshing for obstacle elevation (overhanging hazards vs floor obstacles).
